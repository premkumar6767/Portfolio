About Me

An early-career engineer who enjoys building software, interfaces, and understanding the systems underneath.

I'm currently a Graduate Engineer at BW Design Group, where my work spans building applications and operator interfaces on the Ignition platform — including screen-level development, UI implementation, and exposure to areas like BMS, EPMS and SCADA-related application work. My tasks vary by project rather than being one fixed responsibility.

What I enjoy most is taking a design and bringing it accurately to life as a real, working application — paying attention to visual structure, layout and how an interface should actually behave. I'm still exploring my long-term specialization, and I like learning by building things end to end.

I use AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT and Copilot as development assistants — to research, debug and move faster — while owning the requirements, decisions, testing and implementation myself.
